Event Details

What to expect at each tournament

📝

Registration

Registration opens 6 weeks before each event and closes one week prior to the tournament date. Members receive a 48-hour early registration window.

💰

Entry Fees

Standard events: $75 members / $100 non-members. Majors: $125 members / $175 non-members. Scramble events: $50 per player. All fees include green fees and prizes.

🏆

Prizes

Each event features gross and net prizes, closest-to-the-pin, and longest drive competitions. The season finale awards the coveted Galunga Trophy and champion's jacket.
